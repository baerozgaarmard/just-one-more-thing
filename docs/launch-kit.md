## Reddit — r/freelance / r/webdev / r/SideProject

**Title:** My client turned a 5-week project into 9 weeks with 11 "small
requests". So I built a ledger that prices every "just one more thing".

**Body:**
Every freelancer knows the sentence: "can you just add one small thing?"
Last year a project I quoted at 5 weeks shipped in 9 — not because the client
was difficult, but because nothing was ever written down. 11 small requests,
all "free", all invisible.

I went looking for how other freelancers handle it and the same system kept
coming up: sort every request into Included / Clarify / Swap / Extra / Phase 2,
quote extras separately, and get approval *in writing* before building.

So I built that system as a small app — **Just One More Thing**:

- Log every request the moment it arrives
- Sort it into one of the five buckets
- Extras get auto-quoted (hours × your rate) with a "creep meter" showing
  extras as a % of the original project value
- It writes the client reply for you (warm or firm) — copy, WhatsApp or email
- Printable change-order document = your paper trail
- Local-first: your client data never leaves your browser. No account.
- Installable PWA, works offline

It's free and open source. I built it for my own client work first
(BrandForge Media), and I use the change-order flow on real projects now.

App: https://just-one-more-thing-eight.vercel.app/
Code: https://github.com/baerozgaarmard/just-one-more-thing

What am I missing? What does your worst scope-creep story look like?
